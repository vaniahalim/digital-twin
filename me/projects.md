# Featured Projects

## Passport Pixel
Interactive travel journaling web app with retro pixel-art aesthetic. Users log and visualize trips with authentication and a personalized travel dashboard.
- Live: https://passport-pixel.lovable.app/

## The Curation
Strategic intelligence briefing platform curating high-signal content across money, power, geopolitics, career strategy, technology & AI, and self-mastery. Includes an AI updates feed and curated library.
- Live: https://the-curation.lovable.app

## signSpeak
Translated American Sign Language (ASL) to text and speech through Computer Vision. Built at CMU Hack112; won Social Good Category prize.
- Demo: https://youtu.be/e7JpYViUqVc
- GitHub: https://github.com/vaniahalim/hack112

## SafeT
Fashionable wearable technology that signals for help. 3D-printed prototype with Arduino. Built at CMU TartanHacks; placed Top 5.
- GitHub: https://github.com/vaniahalim/tartanhacks2022-weirdoughs

## La Barista
Python cafe game using OpenCV, A* pathfinding, and Tkinter.
- Demo: https://youtu.be/bOJUl3a9Ueo
- GitHub: https://github.com/vaniahalim/15112-TP

## Augmented Reality Navigation Application
Unity mobile app integrating GPS and SLAM for in-campus navigation. Used ARCore SDK and Mapbox for route generation. Won Bronze at Singapore Science and Engineering Forum.
